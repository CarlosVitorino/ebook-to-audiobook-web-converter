Questions

Which books work?
DRM-free EPUB and text PDF files, in English. Kindle files, DRM-protected books and scanned PDFs are rejected.
How long does it take?
Your audiobook is ready long before you could finish listening to it: about 40 minutes for The Great Gatsby's 4 hours. We email you when it's done.
Can I skip the copyright page and the acknowledgements?
Yes. Before converting you see every section of the book and untick what you don't want to hear.
What happens to my file?
Your upload is deleted as soon as the audiobook is made, and the audiobook {{ keep | int }} hours after it's ready.
